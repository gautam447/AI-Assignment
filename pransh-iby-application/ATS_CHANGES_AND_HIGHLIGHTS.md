## What was breaking ATS on the old resume

| Issue | Why it fails | What we did |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IIM logo and 400+ graphic lines | Parsers skip images; some dump the page as empty | Removed all logos, shapes, and shading |
| Nested tables with shaded header cells | Jobscan / Taleo / Greenhouse often read tables left-to-right and scramble Education | Single column, no tables |
| `O p e r a t i o n s M a n a g e r` letter-spacing | ATS indexes “O p e r a t i o n s” as broken tokens, so “Operations Manager” never matches | Normal words only |
| Name and contact in header/footer | Many ATS ignore headers and footers | Name, email, address in the body at the top |
| No Skills section | Keyword match score collapses | Dedicated Skills block with JD language |
| Headline was four unrelated job titles | Looks like a scattershot apply; none of them is this role | One target line: AI Consultant (AI-Native New Business Development) |
| Duplicate CGPA in POR + Achievements + Certifications | Wastes parse slots and looks padded | Each fact appears once |
| Grammar that parsers still store | “a standardized … workflows”, “while the eliminating standardizing” | Clean, one-idea bullets |
| Dates as `June’24` | Some systems miss the year | `June 2024 – March 2026` |
| No Professional Summary | Recruiter + ATS both need a keyword-dense first block | 4-line summary |
